# Third-party notices

## Public Sans

The unmodified Public Sans font subsets in `fonts/` are **not relicensed under MIT**. They retain the copyright notices and SIL Open Font License in [`fonts/OFL-Public-Sans.txt`](fonts/OFL-Public-Sans.txt).

Normal and italic are variable Latin subsets for weights 400–700, originally obtained from Google Fonts Public Sans v21. Current files, upstream URLs, and checksums are documented in [`fonts/README.md`](fonts/README.md). They do not establish coverage of every language or script.

The typographic PNG artwork is newly authored work, not a font binary. Its source uses Public Sans and the Open Index palette. No other artwork, stock images, or generative image-model output is included.

The earlier CIBC branding kit and its assets are not included in this repository. Its separate licences and authorship are not changed by publication of this foundation.
